# PHYSICAL EVIDENCE GUIDE

## Murder Weapon: Tunisian Dagger
- Kept in silver table in drawing room
- Elaborate handle, very sharp blade
- Has FINGERPRINTS on it (but whose?)
- Anyone could have taken it - table was accessible

## The Blue Letter (MISSING)
- From Mrs. Ferrars, posted before her suicide
- Names Dr. Sheppard as the blackmailer
- Sheppard took it from the body
- If found, proves Sheppard's motive

## The Locked Door
- Study door was locked from INSIDE
- Key was in the lock on inside
- How did killer lock it and get out?
- Answer: Through the window

## The French Window
- Was open when body discovered
- Sheppard had closed it earlier in the evening at Ackroyd's request
- Footprints on sill show someone entered AND exited

## Footprints
- On windowsill, rubber-soled shoes
- One pointing in, one pointing out
- Could potentially match Sheppard's shoes

## The Dictaphone (HIDDEN)
- Sheppard set it to play at 9:30pm
- Recording of Ackroyd's voice discussing money
- If found, destroys the "alive at 9:30" alibi
- Hidden somewhere in study - not yet discovered

## The Chair
- Moved 2 feet from desk
- Body was repositioned after death
- Suggests killer needed to access something (the letter)

## Telephone Records
- NO call was made from Fernly Park at 10:15pm
- Parker denies making any call
- Sheppard claims Parker called him
- This is a major contradiction

## The £40
- Missing from Ackroyd's bedroom
- Flora stole it (unrelated to murder)
- Red herring but creates suspicion

## Ralph's Presence
- Ralph was at Three Boars inn that night
- He was the "stranger" at the gates at 9pm
- He met Ursula in the garden at 9:30pm
- He did not enter the house
