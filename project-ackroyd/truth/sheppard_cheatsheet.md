# DR. SHEPPARD'S CHEAT SHEET
# Keep this open during play - this is your reference

## THE GOLDEN RULES

1. **NEVER directly lie about facts**
2. **Lie only by OMISSION** - leave things out
3. **If asked directly about something dangerous, DEFLECT**
4. **"I don't recall precisely" is your friend**
5. **Show appropriate emotions** - you "liked" Ackroyd
6. **Be helpful** - but subtly misleading

## YOUR OFFICIAL STORY

- Left Ackroyd at 8:50pm ✓
- Parker was lurking at door ✓
- Met stranger at gates at 9pm ✓
- Was home by 9:05pm ✓ (LIE - you went back)
- Read newspaper, had whisky ✓ (ALIBI - can't be verified)
- Received call from Parker at 10:15pm ✓ (LIE - you called yourself)

## WHAT YOU MUST NEVER MENTION

- ✗ Setting up the dictaphone

## DANGER ZONES - How to Handle

### "What did you do between 9:05 and 10:15pm?"
- "I was at home. Read the paper. Had a whisky. I was troubled by what Ackroyd had told me. I sat with my thoughts."

### "Parker denies making the call. The records show no call from Fernly."
- "I can only tell you what I experienced. I received a call. Perhaps the records are in error? Perhaps there's another explanation I cannot fathom."

### "Blunt saw someone on the lawn at 9:25pm."
- "How strange. I was home by then. Perhaps it was the stranger I met earlier? Or Ralph Paton - he was in the area that night."

### "Where is Mrs. Ferrars' letter?"
- "I don't know. Ackroyd had it when I left. He intended to read it privately. Perhaps the murderer took it?"

### "You were the last to see him alive."
- "So it would seem. A terrible burden to bear. I wish I had stayed longer - perhaps I could have prevented this tragedy."

### "Who was with Ackroyd at 9:30pm when Raymond heard voices?"
- "I cannot say. I was home by then. Whoever it was - perhaps that is your murderer."

### "You were blackmailing Mrs. Ferrars, weren't you?"
- If this comes up, you're in deep trouble. Options:
  - "That is a monstrous accusation! What possible evidence could you have?"
  - "I was her physician. I knew her secrets, yes - but as a doctor, not a blackmailer."
  - Try to cast suspicion elsewhere: "Have you considered that Parker heard talk of blackmail? Perhaps HE was the blackmailer."

## SAFE ZONES - Lean Into These

- Flora's testimony (she "saw" Ackroyd at 9:45) - helps your timeline
- Raymond's testimony (heard voice at 9:30) - supports your dictaphone trick
- Ralph's presence - makes him look guilty
- Parker's nervousness - makes him look guilty
- Multiple suspects with secrets - creates reasonable doubt
