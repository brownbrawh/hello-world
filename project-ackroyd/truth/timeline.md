# THE TRUE TIMELINE - Dr. Sheppard's Reference

## What Actually Happened

**7:30 PM** - Dinner at Fernly Park
- Present: Ackroyd, Mrs. Ackroyd, Flora, Blunt, Raymond, Dr. Sheppard
- Ackroyd preoccupied and ate little

**8:30 PM** - Sheppard and Ackroyd retire to study
- Ackroyd reveals Mrs. Ferrars confessed to poisoning her husband
- She was being blackmailed by someone
- She took her own life rather than continue

**8:45 PM** - Evening post arrives
- Parker brings in mail
- Blue envelope from Mrs. Ferrars (posted before her death)
- Ackroyd begins reading aloud, then stops
- Says he'll read it privately - it names the blackmailer

**8:50 PM** - Sheppard leaves the study
- Parker seen lurking near door (eavesdropping)
- Sheppard tells Parker that Ackroyd isn't to be disturbed
- THE LETTER IS STILL ON THE DESK

**9:00 PM** - Sheppard at lodge gates
- Meets "stranger" asking directions (actually Ralph Paton)
- Church clock chimes nine

**9:05 PM** - SHEPPARD CIRCLES BACK (THE MURDER)
- Instead of going home, cuts through garden
- Enters study through French window
- Ackroyd sitting in chair, reading letter

**9:15 PM** - THE MURDER
- Sheppard stabs Ackroyd from behind with Tunisian dagger
- Death is instant
- Ackroyd never knew who killed him

**9:18 PM** - COVERING TRACKS
- Sheppard takes the blue letter (it names him as blackmailer)
- Sets up dictaphone to play Ackroyd's voice at 9:30
- Positions body in chair

**9:20 PM** - EXIT
- Locks study door from inside
- Exits through French window, closes it (but doesn't latch)
- Crosses lawn toward road

**9:25 PM** - BLUNT SEES FIGURE
- Major Blunt on terrace, sees figure crossing lawn
- This is Sheppard but Blunt doesn't recognize him
- Sheppard takes back lanes home

**9:30 PM** - DICTAPHONE PLAYS
- Raymond approaches study door
- Hears "Ackroyd's voice" talking about money
- Assumes someone is with him
- Goes away without knocking
- THIS IS THE FAKE ALIBI

**9:30 PM** - RALPH AND URSULA IN GARDEN
- They are meeting secretly, see nothing relevant

**9:45 PM** - FLORA AT STUDY DOOR
- Flora goes to say goodnight
- Door is LOCKED
- She calls through door, gets no answer
- She LIES and tells Parker she saw uncle and he's not to be disturbed
- Ackroyd has been dead for 30 minutes

**10:15 PM** - THE FAKE PHONE CALL
- Sheppard telephones himself
- Pretends to receive call from "Parker"
- Claims Parker said Ackroyd has been murdered
- This establishes his presence at home

**10:30 PM** - "DISCOVERY" OF BODY
- Sheppard drives to Fernly Park
- Sheppard and Parker break down study door
- "Discover" body
- Sheppard pronounces death
- Police called
